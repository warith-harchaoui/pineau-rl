

22. The 22 failures of the around-the-clock resumption, and the fix (28 November 2026, written BEFORE relaunching). From 27 November 4 pm to 28 November 4 am, the 22 remaining runs all failed twice at the first training step (non-finite gradient). Diagnosis (`diag/grad_step.py`, `diag/anomaly.py`): on the GPU, the forward pass is exact (finite log-probabilities, ratio 1.0000), but the backward pass returns NaN in all 290 gradients, from the final normalisation on (`Qwen2RMSNorm`, product `weight * hidden_states`); on the processor, the same sequences give a gradient of norm 6.1. Neither the 4 threads (a run with 2 threads fails the same way), nor attention (`eager` fails the same way), nor the mask, nor the chunk size is the cause; short sequences pass; the libraries and the system have not changed since the 18 successful runs. Another program (a Chatterbox narration from another project) has used the GPU since the afternoon of 27 November, which `c7.sh` did not see; it is the most likely cause, the phenomenon of deviations 10 and 11 (macOS interrupts long GPU computations when the GPU is contended, and an interrupted computation comes back as NaN). It is not proven: the direct proof required suspending that program, which we did not do. Decisions: (a) these 22 failures are void, not results: they do not measure GRPO but a shared GPU; their logs are kept in `logs/void-2026-11-28/` and `logs/c7.fails.void-2026-11-28`, their partial files in `results_void/2026-11-28/`, outside the folder the analyses read; (b) `c7.sh` starts a run only when the GPU is free: no program known to use it (Chatterbox, Whisper, MLX, Ollama, the Gemma reader) and a utilisation under 30% over three readings one minute apart; (c) `grpo.py` writes the norm of every refused attempt and waits one minute between two attempts, changing nothing in the computation; (d) stopping rule: if a run fails while the GPU was free, `c7.sh` stops and the diagnosis above is wrong; we say so and look elsewhere. The analysis fixed in advance (deviation 20 (g) and (h)) does not change.
