The files lm-*.woff2 of this directory are subsets of the Latin Modern OpenType fonts of TeX Live
(lmroman10-regular, lmromandemi10-regular, lmroman10-bold, lmroman12-bold, lmmono10-regular), made by
build_latex.py: the characters of the house figures only, ligatures and kerning kept, outlines unchanged.
As the clause below requests of derived works, the files carry new names; the fonts are declared in the
figures under the CSS families "Latin Modern Roman" (10 pt designs), "Latin Modern Roman Large" (lmroman12-bold, for titles) and "Latin Modern Mono".
